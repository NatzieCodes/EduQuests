EduQuests Privacy Policy

EduQuests stores account information needed to provide multiplayer study rooms: username, email address, block section, password hash, room activity, and gameplay state.

Passwords are stored as salted password hashes and are not stored in plain text. Study materials are processed only to provide quiz and multiplayer features.

We do not sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers. Uploaded study files may be sent to configured AI services only when quiz generation is requested.

Your choices

Users may request account deletion by using the account settings deletion option or by contacting the site administrator. Account deletion removes the stored EduQuests account record.

Contact: add a public administrator email before launch.
